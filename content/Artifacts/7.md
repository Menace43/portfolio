> [!NOTE]+
> ### Introduction
> 
> This artifact is a generative AI storytelling project developed to explore how AI can help businesses address real-world challenges involving product development, marketing, cost, and time. The project examined two fictional companies, Strange Appeal and Project-Hex, and used generative AI tools to transform product concepts into visual and video marketing materials. The project is relevant to my professional portfolio because it demonstrates my ability to connect generative AI capabilities with business strategy rather than using AI only as a content-generation tool.

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Objective
> 
> The objective was to use generative AI to create storytelling and marketing content for products while exploring how AI could reduce experimentation costs, accelerate concept development, and help businesses test ideas before making larger investments.
> 

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Process
> The project was developed by chaining two AI tools, with each step feeding the next:
> 
> #### Tool 1 — Microsoft Copilot Image Generator
> **Inputs:** Prompt of how image should look like 
> **Outputs:** Generation of content image for the brand
> 
> #### Tool 2 — Canva 
  **Inputs:** prompt alongs side copilot image on how the video should turn out
  **Output:** An instagram/ Tiktok ready content that can be used for to gauge user interest in the product

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Tools
> 
> The primary tools used to develop this artifact were:

> - Canva Magic Media : Turns images into a product ready content

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Value Proposition
> 
> #### Unique Value
> 
> This artifact demonstrates my ability to connect generative AI technology with business strategy. Rather than focusing only on producing attractive AI-generated content, I evaluated how AI could address business problems such as startup costs, marketing production, customer identification, product positioning, and concept validation.
> 
> The Project-Hex case particularly demonstrates my ability to recognize the importance of maintaining product consistency when using generative AI. Because the product depended on a specific mechanical design, I had to refine prompts and provide references to preserve important design characteristics such as the rotary mechanism and deployment system.
> 
> #### Relevance
> 
> This artifact supports my value proposition as a business strategy leader who can evaluate emerging AI technologies and identify practical ways to implement them. It demonstrates that I understand AI not simply as a technical capability but as a strategic resource that can improve efficiency, experimentation, and decision-making.
> 
> Also the artifact showcase my use of AI tools to develop prompt that can be used generate content and solve business challenge. The companies are fictional companies showcasing what I can do if allowed to run the company product business strategy and development and the result that can be  generated from that

> [!NOTE]+
> ## Customization for Audience
> 
> ### Adaptations Made
> 
> I designed the artifact around business-oriented audiences rather than presenting AI as a purely technical subject. Each case study connected the AI workflow to a specific business problem and target customer. For example, Project-Hex was positioned toward technicians, maintenance workers, property managers, mechanics, engineers, and other people who value functional everyday-carry products.
> 
> I also included a comparison of the two companies, their problems, AI inputs, transformations, and marketing results to make the strategic value easier to understand.
> 
> ### Relevance
> 
> These adaptations make the artifact useful to business leaders, managers, and potential employers who need to understand how generative AI can support business objectives without requiring them to have deep technical expertise.

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Lessons Learned
> 
> One of my biggest lessons was that generative AI involves an important balance between speed and creativity versus accuracy, consistency, cost, and human oversight. The Project-Hex case demonstrated that AI can produce visually compelling results while unintentionally changing important product characteristics.
> 
> This experience improved my understanding of why human oversight and iterative prompting are essential when implementing generative AI in a business environment.
>
