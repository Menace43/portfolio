> [!NOTE]+
> ### Introduction
> 
> Artificial intelligence (AI) is rapidly becoming an important part of modern business and is being used across many different industries to improve productivity, creativity, decision-making, and customer experiences. This artifact explores the commercial usage of AI across industries and examines how different AI technologies can be applied to real-world business activities. The project was created to demonstrate an understanding of how AI is moving beyond research and becoming a practical business tool.

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ## Objective
> 
> The objective of this artifact was to research and understand how artificial intelligence is being commercially used across industries. A secondary objective was to identify the benefits and potential value AI provides to organizations and to communicate those findings in a clear and accessible presentation.
> 
> Through this project, I wanted to develop a better understanding of how businesses can use AI to increase efficiency, reduce repetitive work, support creativity, and create new products and services.
> 

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Process
> 
> I began the project by researching different ways AI is currently being used in commercial environments. I examined AI applications across multiple industries and considered how specific AI tools address real-world business needs.
> 
> After identifying relevant examples, I organized the information according to the industry or business function in which the technology could be applied. I focused on explaining not only what each AI technology does, but also why a business would find it useful.
> 
> I then developed the information into a presentation. The presentation was organized so that the audience could understand the relationship between the AI technology, its commercial application, and the value it provides. I reviewed and refined the content to make sure the examples were understandable and connected to practical business situations.

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Tools and Technologies Used
> 
> The primary tools and technologies used to create this artifact included:
> 
> - Microsoft PowerPoint for creating and presenting the artifact.
> - Online research sources for investigating commercial AI applications.
> - AI technologies and examples discussed in the presentation, including Canva AI, Suno AI, GitHub Copilot, and Claude.
> - Internet-based resources for gathering information about AI applications and industry use cases.

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Value Proposition
> 
> The unique value of this artifact is that it connects artificial intelligence to practical commercial applications. Instead of viewing AI only as a technological concept, the project demonstrates how organizations can use AI to solve problems, improve workflows, and create value.
> 
> The artifact also demonstrates my ability to research emerging technology, evaluate its practical applications, organize information, and communicate technical concepts to an audience in a business-oriented way.

---

> [!NOTE]+
> ### Relevance
> 
> This artifact is relevant because AI continues to influence how businesses operate and compete. Understanding how organizations can use AI is increasingly important for professionals in technology, business, marketing, creative industries, and many other fields.
> 
> The project can also serve as a resource for others who want to understand how AI can be applied in a commercial environment. By presenting specific examples, the artifact helps connect theoretical knowledge about AI to real-world applications.
