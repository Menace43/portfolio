# Neural Network Understanding Artifact
View Full Report:  [[3.3 Neural Networks Understanding How Artificial Intelligence Learns Report]]

## Introduction

This artifact introduces neural networks in a way that is accessible to readers who may have limited technical experience. The explanation begins with the basic concept before moving into neurons, layers, training, architectures, applications, benefits, and limitations.

The structure is designed to help readers understand not only **what** a neural network is, but also **how** it works and **why** it matters.

## Objective

The objective is to demonstrate my ability to research an AI concept, organize technical information, explain it clearly, and connect the concept to practical applications.

The artifact also demonstrates my ability to translate a complex technical topic into information that can be understood by a broader professional audience.

## Process

I developed this artifact by first reviewing reliable information about neural networks and identifying the concepts most important for a foundational explanation.

I used IBM's neural-network resources as the primary technical reference and reviewed the provided neural-network portfolio example for ideas about organization, visual explanation, and audience-friendly communication.

The content was then organized into a logical progression:

1. Introduction to neural networks.
2. Definition of a neural network.
3. Explanation of artificial neurons.
4. Neural-network architecture.
5. Training and learning.
6. Types of neural networks.
7. Real-world applications.
8. Benefits and limitations.
9. Relationship to deep learning.
10. Professional relevance.

This process helped transform individual research points into a cohesive learning resource.

## Visual Design

Visuals are an important part of this artifact because neural networks can be difficult to understand through text alone.

The recommended visuals include:

- A diagram of an artificial neuron.
- A diagram showing input, hidden, and output layers.
- A diagram showing forward propagation and backpropagation.
- An illustration showing real-world neural-network applications.

These visuals support the written explanation by allowing readers to see how information flows through a neural network.

## Key Learning

The most important lesson from this artifact is that neural networks learn patterns by processing data through interconnected layers and adjusting their parameters based on errors.

Understanding this basic process provides a foundation for understanding more advanced AI technologies, including CNNs, transformers, and many modern deep-learning systems.
