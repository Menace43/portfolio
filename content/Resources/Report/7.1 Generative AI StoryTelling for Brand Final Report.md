## Strange Appeal  - Case Study 1

Company Name : Strange Appeal
Company Product: Boxy T-shirt
### Company Goals 
Sell a minimalist T-shirt at a reasonable price with minimal startup cost.
### Chosen Vertical / Business Problems

The main problem is that Strange Appeal has three competing production strategies:

1. Buy blank shirts → ship them → print using DTG/DTF in the U.S.
2. Manufacture finished shirts overseas with an MOQ.
3. Use pre-orders and only manufacture after receiving orders.
4. Create professional marketing content without spending heavily on photography, models, studios, and video production

**Chosen Production strategy note**

A pre-order model (Option 3 in the assignment) pairs best with a low-cost marketing-first launch: you only need convincing renders and a landing page to validate demand before committing to a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) overseas. This keeps start-up cost near zero and lets the AI-generated marketing assets double as the actual pre-sell page content.

## Project - Hex Case Study 2

Company Name : Project Hex
Company Product: Stainless Steel Key Holder

### Company Goals
Build a premium but affordable everyday-carry (EDC) brand with a distinct mechanism and strong repeat/gift-driven sales

### Chosen Vertical / Business Problems

Project-Hex needs to determine:

- Who actually needs a key holder?
- What types of people carry multiple keys?
- What industries have workers carrying keys?
- What materials should be considered?
- What mechanism could make the product different from ordinary keychains?

The interesting marketing insight is:

> Project-Hex isn't really selling a key holder. It's selling control over a messy collection of keys.

## Challenges & Trade-offs

Although generative AI can reduce the time and cost required to develop marketing concepts, using AI also creates several challenges and trade-offs. The most important issue is that speed and creativity must be balanced with accuracy, consistency, cost, and human oversight.

This is particularly important for Project-Hex because the product depends on a specific mechanical design. An AI image may create an attractive product while changing important details such as the mechanism, proportions, or way the keys deploy. Similarly, Strange Appeal's AI-generated clothing may not accurately represent the actual fabric, fit, stitching, or manufacturing quality.

## Conclusion

Generative AI is most valuable when multiple tools are connected into a workflow that addresses a real business problem. The goal isn't simply to create AI-generated content; it is to reduce experimentation costs, increase speed, and help a company test ideas before making larger investments.
